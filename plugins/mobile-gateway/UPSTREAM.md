## Why this fork

Harden the gateway before any phone connects to it — loopback-only by default, an owner switch, TLS
with QR-pinned certificate, a `companion-v1` operation allowlist, approvals bound to a hash of the
tool arguments, `engines.dsh`, and log redaction. Full scope and rationale:
[`../../specs/001-gateway-hardening/spec.md`](../../specs/001-gateway-hardening/spec.md) (plan:
[`../../specs/001-gateway-hardening/plan.md`](../../specs/001-gateway-hardening/plan.md)).
